# 0.1.0 — Research preview

Initial public packaging of a 151M geometric residual language model and controlled local-update workflow.

Included: offline tokenization, tensor-only checkpoint loading, restricted acquisition and preservation-assisted consolidation, generated evaluation outputs, frozen-partition checks, review-only decisions, explicit version selection and rollback, CPU engineering tests, and selected historical evidence including negative results.

The separate `gcl-reader-consolidated-79701.zip` model asset contains the previously frozen demonstration checkpoint. It is not a later checking-study winner. Checksums and provenance are documented.

Scope: partial research prototype, not production continual memory. Multi-record binding, unfamiliar construction transfer, broad retention, matched architecture advantage and autonomous growth benefits remain unestablished. Historical result inspection and local engineering reproduction are supported; complete formation/table reproduction is not bundled.

The public API and artifact format may evolve. Keep source and model manifest hashes with every experiment. No original research checkpoint was overwritten in preparing this release.
